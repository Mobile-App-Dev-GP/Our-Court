# Evaluation of top 3 ideas

### 1. Tennis Court Tracker — Our Court

- Mobile: Uses GPS and location services to help users find nearby tennis courts, view court information, and navigate to locations.

- Story: Tennis players often struggle to find accessible courts, determine court conditions, and connect with other players. This app makes that process easier.

- Market: Targets recreational tennis players, beginners, competitive players, and tennis communities looking for accessible courts and playing opportunities.

- Habit: Users can regularly check court availability, sign up to play, communicate with other players, and track courts they've visited.

- Scope: The initial version will focus on court listings, search and filters, court details, and basic community features. Advanced matchmaking and real-time messaging can be added later.

## 2. Digital Clothing Catalog

- Mobile: Uses a smartphone's camera to photograph clothing and create a digital wardrobe that users can access anywhere.

- Story: People often struggle to organize their clothing, remember what they own, and decide what to wear. This app simplifies wardrobe management and outfit planning.

- Market: Targets students, fashion enthusiasts, professionals, and individuals interested in organizing their wardrobes and improving their personal style.

- Habit: Users can check their digital wardrobe daily, plan outfits for upcoming events, and add new clothing whenever they make purchases.

- Scope: The initial version will include clothing uploads, item categories, wardrobe browsing, and basic outfit creation. AI styling recommendations can be added in future versions.

## 3. DJ Song Request App

- Mobile: Allows event attendees to submit song requests from their smartphones while DJs receive and manage requests through a mobile interface.

- Story: Guests often struggle to communicate song requests to DJs during crowded events. This app provides a simple way to request music without interrupting the performance.

- Market: Targets DJs, event organizers, party hosts, college students, and entertainment venues that want more interactive music experiences.

- Habit: Guests can use the app throughout events to request songs, while DJs can use it regularly across different performances to manage audience requests.

- Scope: The initial version will include event creation, song request submission, and a DJ request queue. Features such as voting, Spotify integration, and live popularity rankings can be added later.

## App Decision. 
Tennis Court Tracker 

# Reason for app decision 

### Final App Decision: Tennis Court Tracker — Our Court

Our group ultimately decided to move forward with Our Court, a Tennis Court Tracker, because we felt it was the most unique and innovative idea we had brainstormed. 

We had never come across a tennis court tracking app that prioritized helping people find and build communities through the sport. We wanted to create an app that not only helps users locate tennis courts but also encourages them to connect with others, explore new locations, and create fun, memorable experiences. 

Additionally, we envisioned incorporating game-like features that allow users to track the courts they frequently visit, discover new playing locations, and build a personal history of their tennis experiences. 

Ultimately, we chose Our Court because it combines sports, community, exploration, and entertainment into one engaging mobile application.
